# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a potential security vulnerability in Humanity Ledger, please do NOT report it on the public issue tracker.

Instead, please email us directly at **humanityledger@icloud.com**. 
*(Note: As we are currently in beta, response times may vary, but we will make every effort to review reports promptly).*

## Current Scope
The Humanity Ledger is currently a **Web2 Prototype**. Bug bounty programs and formal audits for our smart contracts and ZK circuits will be announced when we transition to our production Aztec L2 architecture.

Please do not submit reports regarding the simulated nature of our current ZK implementation, as this is a known architectural state documented in `docs/STATUS.md`.
